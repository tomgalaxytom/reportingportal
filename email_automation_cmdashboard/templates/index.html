SMTP Guidelines

Gmail Setup

  1. Navigate to your Google Account > Security.
  2. Enable **2-Step Verification**.
  3. Search for **App Passwords**.
  4. Create an App Password for 'Mail', copy the 16-character code, and use it as your password above.

Common Ports

  • 587: Port for STARTTLS (recommended).
  • 465: Port for SSL.
  • 25: Unencrypted SMTP.
